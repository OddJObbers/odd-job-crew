Our Services

Weeding

Fast and thorough garden weeding.

Learn More

Keep your garden looking its best with our thorough weeding service. We remove weeds from garden beds, paths, and around plants by hand or with the right tool.

Lawn Mowing

Clean, tidy lawns all year round.

Learn More

We mow your lawn to a professional standard, including edging around paths, driveways, and garden beds. Whether it's a one-time cut or regular maintenance, we'll leave your lawn looking clean, fresh, and well cared for.

Hedge Trimming

Neat hedges and tidy gardens.

Learn More

We'll trim and shape your hedges to keep them healthy, tidy, and looking their best. We clean up all clippings afterwards, so your property is left spotless.

Leaf Blowing

Fast leaf blowing and outdoor clean-ups.

Learn More

We'll clear leaves, grass clippings, and other outdoor debris from driveways, patios, footpaths, decks, and gardens using a leaf blower, leaving your outdoor areas clean and safe.

Garden Clean-Ups

Complete garden tidy-ups.

Learn More

Need your garden refreshed? We'll remove weeds, trim plants, rake leaves, clear green waste, and tidy garden beds to restore your outdoor space and make it look its best.

Moving Help

Helping move boxes, furniture and more.

Learn More

Need an extra pair of hands? We can help move furniture, boxes, appliances, and other heavy items around your home or during a move. We'll handle your belongings carefully and work efficiently.

Babysitting

Friendly and responsible babysitting.

Learn More

We provide friendly, responsible babysitting in a safe and caring environment. We can supervise children, play games, help with homework, prepare simple meals, and keep them entertained while you're away.
